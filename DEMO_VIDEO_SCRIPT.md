## Recording Tips

1. **Use demo mode** - The $238K portfolio looks impressive
2. **Slow your taps** - Let animations complete, don't rush
3. **Screen record at 60fps** - Smooth animations matter
4. **No voiceover first** - Record screen, add voice after (easier to edit)
5. **Horizontal or vertical?** - Check hackathon requirements, vertical is more authentic for mobile
